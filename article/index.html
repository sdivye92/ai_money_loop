The AI Money Loop

The AI build-out has become a vast financial wager: valuations, capital spending, contracts and debt all assume that demand will keep growing. This series follows the money through 59 companies to show how that wager has spread across the market, where the weakest points sit, and how a shock can travel from one firm to many.
